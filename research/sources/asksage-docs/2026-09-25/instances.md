 Ask Sage Instances 
 Ask Sage runs as several separate, isolated instances to meet different security and compliance needs. Sign in at the URL that matches your organization’s environment.
 SaaS instances 
 Instance | URL | Environment | Compliance | 
 Commercial | chat.asksage.com | Commercial | Not FedRAMP | 
 Government | chat.asksage.ai | U.S. Government | FedRAMP (Class D) | 
 Department of Defense | chat.asksage.mil | Department of Defense | DoD Impact Level 5 (IL5) | 
 Other instances 
 Ask Sage also runs additional hosted and self‑hosted (on‑premises and air‑gapped Edge) instances for specific organizations. If your organization uses a dedicated instance, its URL is provided by your organization’s Ask Sage administrator.
 Not sure which instance to use? Contact your Ask Sage administrator or support@asksage.ai.
